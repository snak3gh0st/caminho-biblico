Historical and audiovisual context

The dossiers distinguish the narrated time from the composition of the book. Each observation identifies the notes that support it and differentiates affirmation from the source, interpretation and debate. A statement by a commentator does not amount to independent historical proof. The notes consulted are selective.

João 4's video is an AI-generated image montage with camera movement and synthetic OpenAI voice. The captions were aligned by automatic transcription. The script follows the study and the sources of the chapter.

Cross-references are suggestions for comparison. The existence of a link between two verses does not in itself demonstrate that one confirms the interpretation of the other. The routes on the map unite only places cited in narrative sequence; the lines do not correspond to historical roads.

Comparisons in the portal use World English Bible, in the public domain.

Words heard in John 4

Words are bridges to Hebrew texts. The Gospel of John came to us in Greek. The recordings are from contemporary speakers and were converted into MP3. Beér, dafna3.bot, sadê, Ijon and Emuná, Ijon have a CC0 license. The MP3 version of malyim, recorded by Buffer via Lingua Libre, maintains the license CC BY - SA 4.0.
